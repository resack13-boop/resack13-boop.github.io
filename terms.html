Perevertysh Backup is provided for the personal use of its operator to back up VPS files into a Google Drive account the operator controls. It is not offered as a service to third parties.

The operator is responsible for securing OAuth credentials, verifying backup integrity, managing retention, and complying with Google Drive and rclone terms. Availability and storage are subject to those providers' policies.

Nothing on this site authorizes public access to backup files, trading credentials, or the underlying VPS. For questions, visit the GitHub repository.
